## Priority Issues

**[P3] A few residual font-size literals don't reference the token system**
- Why it matters: `.pager-group` (`TopicPager.astro:107`, 0.75rem), `.search-kbd` (`SearchBox.astro:109`, 0.8rem), and `.diagram-tree` (`global.css:228`, 0.9rem — an exact duplicate of `--font-size-sm`) are small, low-visibility values the font-size cleanup pass didn't reach. Not a visual defect; a maintainability gap that could silently re-multiply if left as the copy-paste source for the next contributor.
- Fix: point `.diagram-tree` at `var(--font-size-sm)` directly (values are identical); decide whether 0.75rem/0.8rem deserve a dedicated `--font-size-xs` token or should round up to the existing `--font-size-sm` (0.9rem).
- Suggested command: `$impeccable typeset`

**[P3] Homepage bento grid has no explicit ceiling as exercise levels grow**
- Why it matters: the grid currently renders 1 accent + 4 shaded cells (5 total, at the edge of the ≤4-choices cognitive-load guideline but semantically justified since levels map 1:1 to real curriculum tiers). `discoverExercises()` will silently add a 6th tile the moment a 5th level's exercises are published, with no code path forcing a design decision at that point.
- Fix: not urgent; consider a short comment in `index.astro` flagging the assumption, or a design decision now for what happens past 5 tiles.
- Suggested command: `$impeccable layout`
