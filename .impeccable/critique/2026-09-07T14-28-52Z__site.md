## Priority Issues

**[P1] KaTeX inline formula overflows its container on theory pages**
- Why it matters: this happens on the exact surface the product's primary persona (a student revising the night before an exam) uses most, inside the 820px reading "Measure" column. A bolded inline example ("Ejemplo: 3x-5=7") overflowing by 19px risks visual clipping or overlap on narrower viewports, directly undermining the "find a formula in seconds" goal.
- Fix: audit `.description` / KaTeX inline rendering in `src/pages/theory/[slug].astro` and `PropertyBox.astro` for `overflow-wrap`/`white-space` handling on bolded inline math; verify across a few long-formula topics, not just algebra.
- Suggested command: `$impeccable audit`

**[P2] Shadow-elevation vocabulary is violated on hover across multiple components**
- Why it matters: DESIGN.md defines a fixed 4-step shadow vocabulary (5/3/2/1px) and calls a 5th step "a violation," yet hover states on the homepage bento cells, `.pdf-link`, `.action-btn` (404), and `.pager-card` (TopicPager) each grow to different, undocumented offsets (4px, 6px, 7px). Rest-state values were independently verified correct; the drift is specifically in hover growth.
- Fix: normalize every card/button hover to grow to exactly 5px5px0 (or if `+2px` growth is the actual intended rule, codify it in DESIGN.md and apply uniformly).
- Suggested command: `$impeccable polish`

**[P2] Typography scale has drifted from its documented ramp**
- Why it matters: 12 genuine undocumented font-size outliers (across `404.astro`, `index.astro`, `theory/[slug].astro`, `exercises/[level].astro`, `Breadcrumbs.astro`, `LevelTags.astro`, `SearchBox.astro`, `TopicPager.astro`) plus one label using `0.13em` letter-spacing against a documented `0.08em` spec suggest the type scale is being extended ad hoc rather than through the documented system, undermining "Consistency and Standards."
- Fix: either fold the recurring outlier sizes into the documented ramp as new named tokens, or bring them back onto the existing scale.
- Suggested command: `$impeccable typeset`

**[P2] The homepage's own signature bento component is missing its documented third variant**
- Why it matters: DESIGN.md's bento spec calls for "one full-ink-fill spanning 2 cols, one 9%-tint, rest shaded surface." The shipped homepage only has two cell types (full-ink and shaded); `--accent-tint` is defined twice in `global.css` and referenced nowhere in the codebase — a designed variant that was never wired in.
- Fix: either apply `--accent-tint` to one bento cell, or remove the dead token and update DESIGN.md to describe the 2-variant reality.
- Suggested command: `$impeccable polish`

**[P3] Mobile menu does not manage focus on open or close**
- Why it matters: acceptable under the disclosure-widget pattern, but a keyboard user opening the full-screen menu has to Tab an extra step to reach content, and Escape drops focus to `<body>` rather than back to the hamburger trigger — a small, avoidable disorientation for keyboard/screen-reader users.
- Fix: move focus to the first menu link on open; restore focus to the trigger button on close.
- Suggested command: `$impeccable audit`

## Minor Observations

- 8 elements (in the persistent nav/menu component, present on every page) transition `margin` rather than `transform`, which is a layout-triggering property — a performance-oriented nit rather than a visual defect, but worth switching to `transform`/`max-height` variants if scroll-jank is ever reported.
- Footer copyright reads "© 2026 Ágora Academy" (English word order) while the rest of the site consistently says "Academia Ágora" — a small brand-name inconsistency worth normalizing.
- `.meta-label` (the "eyebrow" utility class) is reused broadly for pager direction, PDF counts, formula labels, and section counts — functioning as a general badge style rather than the more restrained "earned eyebrow" DESIGN.md describes. Not a literal violation (none of these are decorative section eyebrows), but worth a naming/intent check.
- Detector overlay UI itself (not site code) uses blurred shadows and rounded corners — expected, that's the third-party tool's own chrome, not a site violation.
