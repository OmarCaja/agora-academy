# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, confirmed as equally primary, doing genuinely different jobs on the same site.

**Parents in Cuenca evaluating where to send their child for maths support.** They arrive comparing options, with a trust question and little patience. Their path is evaluate, then make contact. They are the ones who decide and who pay.

**Enrolled students (Primaria, E.S.O., Bachillerato).** They return during the week to revise. They come for one specific formula, topic or exercise sheet, frequently on a phone, frequently the night before an exam, and frequently in order to print something. Their path is find, then study.

Neither path may obstruct the other.

## Product Purpose

Ágora is a maths tutoring academy in Cuenca, Spain. The site exists to do two things: give a parent enough confidence to phone or email, and act as the day-to-day study resource for students already enrolled.

Success is a parent making contact, and an enrolled student finding what they came for without having to ask anyone.

## Positioning

Small groups with individual attention and close tracking of each student's progress.

The academy also maintains a free, openly accessible study library: 18 theory topics with rendered formulas and worked examples, plus exercise PDFs organised by school level. **Per the owner, this library exists to serve enrolled students, not to acquire new ones.** Growth comes from word of mouth. Future work should not be justified on the grounds that the library is a marketing funnel.

That said, the library is publicly indexed and carries structured data, so its URLs and anchors have search value that must not be broken casually. Serving students is the purpose; discoverability is a byproduct worth protecting.

## Operating Context

- Students read theory pages on mobile, often shortly before an exam, and use the in-page search to jump straight to a single formula.
- Exercise material is distributed as PDFs, opened in a new tab and often printed. The site ships a dedicated print stylesheet that hides navigation and prevents formula cards from breaking across pages. **Printing is a supported workflow, not an afterthought.**
- Everything is in Spanish (`lang="es"`). Level names follow the Spanish system: Primaria, E.S.O. (1º to 4º), Bachillerato (1º to 2º).
- The academy is a physical place: Calle Fermín Caballero, 3 (edificio Máster), Planta 6 [thegoodspace], Cuenca. Dropping by is a real route.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static Astro site, built on push to `main`, deployed to GitHub Pages at `www.agoraacademy.es`. **No server, no database, no backend of any kind.**
- **No forms anywhere, by design.** Enquiries arrive by phone (649 021 173) or email (info@agoraacademy.es) only. A contact form would require a third-party endpoint and is not wanted.
- Content is auto-discovered at build time. Theory topics come from `src/content/topics/*.json`, validated by a Zod schema. Exercise pages come from the `public/ejercicios/<level>/<topic>/*.pdf` filesystem tree. Adding a JSON file or dropping in a PDF is sufficient to publish; no routing changes are needed.
- Maths renders through KaTeX. Explanatory graphs are produced by committed Python scripts into `public/ejemplos/`.
- Current content: 18 theory topics, 27 exercise PDFs across 4 published levels (1º, 3º and 4º E.S.O., 1º Bachillerato), and 33 generated graphs. 2º E.S.O. and 2º Bachillerato are configured in `levelOrder` but have no content yet.
- **URL slugs and in-page anchors are derived from content titles.** Section anchors are generated by `slugify()` from Spanish headings, so renaming a section title silently breaks deep links and the table of contents.
- **Opening hours are not fixed and vary.** The site must not assert specific hours anywhere, including structured data.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Ágora / Academia Ágora. The `ÁGORA` wordmark and the existing SVG logo glyph are fixed and must not be redrawn.
- Voice: plain, factual Spanish. No marketing filler, no superlatives, no invented warmth.
- Language: Spanish throughout, for every audience.
- Light and dark themes are both first-class and follow the visitor's system preference.
- An established visual system already exists in code (design tokens in `src/styles/global.css`, components in `src/components/`). It is the incumbent design authority. It is documented separately, not here.

## Evidence on Hand

- 33 generated explanatory graphs (`public/ejemplos/`)

**Confirmed absent. Future work must never fabricate these:**

- No teacher names, biographies, credentials, qualifications, or headcount

- No testimonials, reviews, ratings, or named students

These are not gaps waiting to be filled with placeholders. Until the owner supplies real material, their absence is the honest state of the product.

## Product Principles

1. **Serve the enrolled student first on content pages.** Someone revising the night before an exam should reach a single formula in seconds, on a phone.
2. **Two audiences, one site, no dilution.** Evaluate-then-contact and find-then-study must both stay short. Neither may be made worse to flatter the other.
3. **Never fabricate credibility.** With no photos, prices, staff names or testimonials, trust is earned only by the honesty and quality of the material itself.
4. **Content is the interface.** Adding a JSON file or a PDF must remain enough to publish. Any change that reintroduces manual routing is a regression.
5. **Preserve slugs, anchors and printability.** Deep links are how returning students navigate, and printing is how exercises get used.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- The audience includes school-age students, so plain language and legibility outrank density.
- Established and to be preserved, not treated as aspirations: WCAG AA contrast in both themes, visible keyboard focus, keyboard-reachable navigation, and full `prefers-reduced-motion` support across all animation.
- Heavy mobile use by students makes small-screen legibility and touch target size a standing requirement.
